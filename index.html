Automated Laboratory Data Ingestion Platform — SFDA

Workflow Automation · Scheduler & Worker Services · Redis · PostgreSQL — SFDA

  • Built a dedicated in-house automation platform for the Saudi Food and Drug Authority (SFDA) that receives laboratory result emails containing large volumes of product data, then automatically opens, reads, parses, validates, and inserts the data into the main database.
  • Replaced a fully manual process, in which staff extracted, analyzed, and entered product records by hand over several days, with near real-time automated ingestion that requires no human data entry.
  • Designed a service-based architecture: a Scheduler triggers ingestion cycles, a Worker processes incoming emails and attachments through a Redis queue, and a Workflow service applies business rules and writes validated records to PostgreSQL, with persistent volumes for both data stores.
  • Developed and hosted the platform internally rather than using third-party automation tools, keeping regulated product data within controlled infrastructure and preventing data leakage.
